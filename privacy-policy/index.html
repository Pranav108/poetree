Poetree / Privacy

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Poetree app and the website at poetree.in. Poetree is offered by Pranav Pratap Singh.

Information in the app

We do not collect personal information through the Poetree app. You can start reading without creating an account or giving us personal details. The app does not show ads or require device permissions just to read poems.

If you save favourite poems or change your reading preferences, the app uses those choices to provide the experience you selected. We do not use your reading choices for advertising.

Visiting this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its pages do not ask you to submit personal information or run an analytics script. As the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical information about visits, such as your IP address and browser information, under its privacy statement.

If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your message and handle your request. Your email provider and ours may also process the message under their own privacy policies. Please do not send sensitive information unless it is necessary for your request.

You can ask us about, or request deletion of, correspondence you sent by emailing the address below.

Changes to this policy

If Poetree's information practices change, we will update this page and the date above. Please check this page for the latest version.

Questions?

Contact Pranav Pratap Singh at pranav.nit.vns@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
